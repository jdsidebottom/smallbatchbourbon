Weekly  ·  Thursday mornings
 

You’re in. Here is the whole promise in one sentence: every Thursday morning you get the week’s verified facts, one honest argument, and one number telling you what a bottle is actually worth.

No unboxings. No countdown timers. No breathless last chance on a bottle that will be back on the shelf by Tuesday.
What’s in an issue
 
• Proof. What actually happened this week. Three to five items, each with a source link. If we can’t source it, it doesn’t run.
• Perspective. One argument, made properly. This is the reason to subscribe, and it is the part we would defend out loud.
• What We’d Pay. One bottle, one number. Not what it “goes for” — what we would hand over at a register without feeling stupid the next morning.
• The Shelf Report. What readers actually paid, and where. Bottle, price, store, city.
The rule
 

Every price, proof, age statement, release date and availability claim gets a primary source, or a dated sighting with a store and a city. A price you saw yourself, on a day you can name, counts. “Someone said it’s going for four hundred” does not.

And when we don’t know something, we say we don’t know. That comes up more often than you would expect. It is most of the difference between this and a forum thread.

One thing before you go. The Shelf Report only works if people send prices. Reply to this email with a single line — bottle, what you paid, the store, your city.

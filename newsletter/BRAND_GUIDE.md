# Proof & Perspective — Production Brand Guide

Version 1.0 — September 2026

## Core idea

Proof & Perspective is the editorial newsletter and podcast identity for SmallBatchBourbon.com.
Voice: intelligent, independent, unfiltered, and anti-snob.

**Primary tagline:** Drink Smarter. Ignore the Noise.

## Locked typography

- **PROOF:** Cormorant Garamond Light 300, horizontally compressed 8%.
- **Ampersand:** Cormorant Garamond Regular 400.
- **PERSPECTIVE:** Oswald Bold 700.
- **Supporting/body:** Inter Regular 400 and Medium 500.
- **Tagline:** Inter Medium 500, uppercase, generous tracking.

All fonts are free Google Fonts and are bundled in `06_Fonts`.

## Logo use

- Use `Signature_Glass` when space permits. The Glencairn sits between “Drink Smarter.” and “Ignore the Noise.”
- Use `Clean_Drop` for tighter spaces or layouts already containing whisky imagery.
- Files named `dark` are designed for dark backgrounds; files named `light` are designed for light backgrounds.
- `mono-black` and `mono-white` are for single-color production.
- The SVGs are editable vector masters. PNGs have transparent backgrounds unless explicitly labeled podcast cover.

## Minimum widths

- Signature with Glencairn: 120 px / 1.0 in.
- Clean with amber drop: 100 px / 0.8 in.
- P&P monogram: 48 px / 0.4 in.
- Amber drop: 32 px / 0.28 in.
- Glencairn: 36 px / 0.3 in.
- Podcast glass: 48 px / 0.4 in.

Clear space around a logo should be at least the cap-height of the ampersand.

## Podcast mark

The Glencairn-with-headphones mark is a formal member of the system. Use the standalone micro-mark for avatars and episode art; use the square cover for podcast-directory artwork.

## Export notes

- 512 px: web UI, email, social thumbnails.
- 1024/1400 px: large digital placements.
- 2048 px: retina and high-resolution digital use.
- 3000 px: print and podcast-directory master artwork.
- SVG: preferred for print, signage, merchandise, and any future resizing.
